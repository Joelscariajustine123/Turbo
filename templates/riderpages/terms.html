The basics of using Turbo Cab. Last updated {% now "j F Y" %}.

Your account

  • Use your own name, a working email address and phone number. Keep your password and verification codes private — Turbo Cab staff will never ask for them.
  • You're responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.

Booking and riding

  • The fare is shown before you book: a base fare plus a fixed rate per kilometre for the vehicle type you choose.
  • Share your ride OTP with the driver only after you are in the vehicle.
  • You pay after the ride is completed, by UPI or cash. For UPI, enter the real transaction ID from your payment app.
  • You can cancel a ride until a driver accepts it.

Driving with Turbo

  • Keep your licence, vehicle and registration details accurate and up to date.
  • Your location is shared while you're online. Mark "Arrived" and "Complete" only at the pickup and destination, and start a ride only with the rider's OTP.
  • Confirm cash payments only once you have received the money.

Fair use

Don't misuse the service — for example by entering false locations or payment details, harassing other users, or trying to access accounts or data that aren't yours. We may suspend accounts that do.
