Privacy policy

What Turbo Cab collects, why, and how long we keep it. Last updated {% now "j F Y" %}.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and username.
  • Ride details: pickup and drop locations (map coordinates and addresses), vehicle type, distance, fare, ride status and times.
  • Driver location: while a driver is online, their device's GPS position is sent every few seconds. Only the latest position is kept for live tracking, plus a sparse trail during rides.
  • Driver profile: licence number, vehicle model, registration number and an optional profile photo.
  • Payments: the payment method you choose (UPI or cash) and, for UPI, the transaction ID you enter. We never see or store bank, card or UPI PIN details.
  • Messages you send through the contact form, and ratings you give drivers.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including emailing one-time verification codes.
  • To match riders with drivers, calculate fares and show live trip tracking.
  • A driver's live location is shown only to the rider of a ride that driver accepted, and only until the ride ends. The rider's phone number is shared with the driver only after they accept the ride.
  • To record payments and driver earnings, and to answer your messages.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Trip GPS trails are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once.
  • Account and ride records are kept while your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove your personal details.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, verification codes and ride OTPs are stored only as keyed hashes, and every page and API checks that you are allowed to see the data you request.

Your choices and contact

You can update your name, email and phone in your account settings, turn location sharing off by going offline (drivers), and ask for a copy or deletion of your data by emailing Turbocabs@gmail.com or using the contact form.
